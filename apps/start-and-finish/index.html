🎯 Start & Finish

12 weeks of small experiments to make starting easier and build trust in your ability to follow through.

The idea

Practise action, not perfection

Fear of failing can make a task feel dangerous; attention and planning difficulties can make it hard to begin even when you care. Neither is a character flaw. This plan gives you a tiny starting step, a way to test worrying thoughts, and a repeatable finish line. “Rewire” here means building habits through practice, not a guaranteed change to your brain in 12 weeks.

Keep this light: choose one project at a time. Aim for 10–20 minutes on four days each week. On a hard day, two minutes counts. If you miss a day, restart with the next small step; never catch up.

Your daily loop

Three steps, about 10 minutes

1. Pick one outcome
Write the next visible action, such as “draft the first three lines.”
2. Set a timer
Start for two minutes. Continue for ten if you can.
3. Leave a breadcrumb
Write exactly where to resume tomorrow.

Weekly review: What did I start? What got in the way? What smaller step will I try next? Count starts and useful attempts, not just completed projects.

When to get more support

ADHD cannot be diagnosed by this plan. If attention, organisation or impulsivity have affected several parts of life for a long time, speak with your GP about an assessment. Anxiety, sleep problems and low mood can also affect focus. You can use these practical supports while seeking an assessment. If fear of failure or anxiety is significantly affecting daily life, NHS talking therapies may help.
